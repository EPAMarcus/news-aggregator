## Tech

### AI
- Mistral released the open weights of Mistral Large 4, claiming the top open model for agentic coding and cyber tasks at lower cost than rivals such as GLM-5.3, and promising smaller versions soon. It strengthens the case for open weights as a hedge against closed-model vendors cutting off access.
- GitHub announced Copilot Fusion, which automatically picks and orchestrates models and reportedly comes close to Claude Code's benchmark results at about half the cost. It also reported 7.4 billion commits in September and a major rebuild of its infrastructure, driven by demand from coding agents.
- Google is opening Android Studio and the Android CLI to third-party coding agents such as Claude Code and Codex, so they can test and fix bugs on their own. Separately, DeepMind's Genie 4 world model offers agents on-demand test environments, though its physics is still unreliable.
- Anthropic filed to go public at a reported valuation of $1.5–2 trillion. The filing shows strong margins and revenue growth, and analysts say its large compute commitments depend on AI spreading beyond coding.
- Experienced developers remain wary of fully agentic development because "built" does not mean "works in production", with red builds and lower delivery stability as examples. Leaders are urged to give them room to experiment rather than label them laggards.

### Security
