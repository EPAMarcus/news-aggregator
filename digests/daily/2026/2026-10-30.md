## SAP

- At SAP TechEd 2026, SAP unveiled tools in Joule Studio and Joule Work for building agents (70+ models, no-code and pro-code, A2A/MCP support). It also announced an agent gateway, a planned Enterprise AI Gateway, an AI Governance Assistant for EU AI Act risk classification, and expanded Knowledge Graph and Business Data Cloud capabilities. DSAG welcomed the added control but called for cost transparency. ([SAP Community – Technology Blogs by SAP 1](<https://community.sap.com/t5/technology-blog-posts-by-sap/joule-studio-empowers-teams-to-build-trusted-custom-agentic-solutions/ba-p/14493943>), [SAP Community – Technology Blogs by SAP 2](<https://community.sap.com/t5/technology-blog-posts-by-sap/agentic-ai-for-abap-development-what-s-new-at-sap-teched-2026/ba-p/14512694>), [SAPinsider](<https://sapinsider.org/blogs/sap-teched-2026-new-tools-to-build-and-govern-ai-agents/>), [E3 Magazine](<https://e3mag.com/de/dsag-begruesst-neue-ki-kontrollen-und-fordert-kostentransparenz/>))
- SAP is positioning itself as the orchestration and governance layer for "physical AI", linking robots to business data and audit trails, with early deployments at Arçelik-LG, Intel and Holcim. SAPinsider's research suggests many customers are not yet ready to govern AI in SAP processes. ([SAPinsider](<https://sapinsider.org/blogs/sap-takes-physical-ai-into-the-real-world/>))

SAP and NTT also plan to cooperate on sovereign cloud (SAP Sovereign Cloud On-Site) and, later, business AI. ## Science

- Studies suggest climate feedbacks such as thawing permafrost, wildfires and wetlands could add 0.2–0.4 °C of warming this century. These effects are missing from most climate models, so the time before key thresholds are breached may be shorter than assumed. ([MIT Technology Review](<https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/10/29/1147735/climate-models-blind-spot/>))

## Health

- A JAMA Psychiatry study finds the global incidence of anorexia and bulimia rose 71% between 1990 and 2023, and clinicians warn that GLP-1 weight-loss drugs may worsen the problem. New guidelines recommend screening for eating disorders before prescribing them.
